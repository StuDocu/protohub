⇉ ONE TO CLASS Exam exchange moonshot

Twenty-one days out, students say which topics they're weak and strong on. Each writes one worked solution for a classmate, and a different classmate writes one for them — all revealed at T-7. The closing date does the recruiting: to receive, you have to be in before sign-ups shut.

The question from the thread

Do we understand where students work together?

Honest answer, in three parts.

Offline: no research exists. Nothing on campus or in-person study anywhere in the mission record. #9 is the proposal to fix that.
Online: everything we have is one-to-one, not class-wide. Asking a classmate for material, uploading for classmates. Nothing where one student reaches the whole class.
From our own student conversations. Co-studying is real but inconsistent — some do it in person, some only before exams, some never.
